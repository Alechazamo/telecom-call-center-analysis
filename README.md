# telecom-call-center-analysis
## Business Problem

The company was experiencing a high number of missed and unanswered inbound and outbound calls, which negatively affected key performance indicators (KPIs) and the overall efficiency of its customer service operations. To improve performance and service quality, the company needed to identify operators with consistently low performance and determine whether they required additional support or training.

## Objective

The objective of this project was to identify inefficient call center operators by analyzing operational performance metrics and comparing their behavior with efficient operators. The analysis aimed to uncover performance patterns and support data-driven decisions to improve operational efficiency and customer service quality.

## Dataset

The dataset contains operational data from a telecommunications call center, including information about inbound and outbound calls, missed calls, waiting times, call durations, operator IDs, and other performance-related metrics used to evaluate operator efficiency.

## Tools

  ## Methodology

- Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Comparison between efficient and inefficient operators.
- Visualization of operational metrics.
- Statistical hypothesis testing to validate differences between groups.

- ## Key Findings

The analysis revealed clear performance differences between efficient and inefficient operators. Inefficient operators showed less favorable operational metrics, including higher rates of missed calls and significant differences in key performance indicators. Statistical testing confirmed that these differences were not due to random variation, supporting the identification of operators requiring additional attention.

## Statistical Analysis

A statistical hypothesis test was performed using SciPy to compare efficient and inefficient operators. The results indicated statistically significant differences between both groups, providing evidence to support the conclusions drawn from the exploratory analysis.

## Conclusions

This analysis demonstrates how data can be used to identify operational inefficiencies and support decision-making within a call center environment. The findings can help managers prioritize coaching, training, or process improvements for operators with lower performance, ultimately contributing to better customer service and operational efficiency.
